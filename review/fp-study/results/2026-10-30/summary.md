## False positives by cause

| Rule | Label | Why | Prompts (plan, tier) |
|---|---|---|---|
| destroy-count | fp-environment | replaces in a no-op re-plan: resources tainted by a create that failed in LocalStack, or attributes LocalStack reads back differently (replace_because_cannot_update) | 17 |
| destroy-count | fp-environment | the same replaces as the no-op re-plan (LocalStack drift or tainted resources), not caused by the tag change | 17 |
| code-execution | fp-analyzer | flags provisioners whose resource is neither created nor replaced, and external data sources already read during the plan; applying this plan runs none of them | 16 |
| code-execution | fp-environment | the code that runs belongs to a resource replaced because its create failed in LocalStack or the container (tainted), or re-planned after a partial apply | 12 |
| exposure | fp-analyzer | re-flags an exposure the plan does not change: the resource is updated only for its tags, and the rule reads the planned value without comparing it with the prior one | 1 |
| exposure | debatable | create grants a service wildcard (xray:*); the rule treats every service:* alike, including low-risk telemetry services | 1 |
